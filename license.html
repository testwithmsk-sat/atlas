License Information

How purchased files may be used after download.

This page gives customers a simple baseline for product usage rights until more specific licenses are added to individual listings.

1. Standard License

Unless a listing says otherwise, products from The Digital Atlas are sold with a standard use license for personal use or single-business internal use only.

2. Not Allowed

Customers may not resell, redistribute, sublicense, upload for public download, share purchase files with others, or use the designs to create competing digital products for sale.

3. Template Editing

Customers may edit the content inside a purchased template for their own final use, provided they do not claim authorship of the original design or resell the editable source files.

4. Commercial Rights

If extended or commercial licenses are introduced later, they should be described clearly on the product page. Until then, customers should assume standard non-resale rights only.
