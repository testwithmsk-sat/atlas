Wedding Checklist

Use this page as your master checklist while planning. Check items off as you go.

12+ Months Before

  • Set your wedding date
  • Decide on your total budget
  • Draft your guest list
  • Choose your wedding style or theme
  • Research and book your venue

9-12 Months Before

  • Book photographer and videographer
  • Book caterer and entertainment
  • Start shopping for wedding attire
  • Choose your wedding party
  • Research florist and decor ideas

6-9 Months Before

  • Order attire and accessories
  • Book florist, beauty team, and transport
  • Plan ceremony details
  • Create invitation or wedding website plan
  • Start honeymoon planning

3-6 Months Before

  • Send save the dates or invitations
  • Finalize your guest list
  • Book pre-wedding events
  • Plan seating chart and menu
  • Schedule dress fittings

1-3 Months Before

  • Confirm all vendors and timelines
  • Create the wedding day schedule
  • Prepare signage, favors, and final details
  • Review payments and balances due
  • Pack essentials and emergency kit

Final Week

  • Confirm RSVPs and guest count
  • Finalize seating chart
  • Prepare vendor tips and envelopes
  • Steam attire and pack accessories
  • Rest, hydrate, and enjoy the moment
