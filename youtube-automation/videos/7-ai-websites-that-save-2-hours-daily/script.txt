Hook:
If your day disappears into writing, searching, summarizing, and making slides, these 7 AI websites can help you win back serious time.

Main sections:
1. Intro
This video covers 7 AI websites that are practical for students, creators, and freelancers. The goal is not to automate your brain. The goal is to cut repetitive work and move faster.

2. ChatGPT
Use ChatGPT for idea generation, first drafts, rewrite help, study plans, and outlining. It is best when you give it a clear task and enough context.

3. Perplexity
Use Perplexity when you want quick research with linked sources. It is especially useful when you are trying to understand a topic quickly and need a starting point.

4. Grammarly
Use Grammarly to clean up writing across emails, assignments, and applications. Faster editing means less time fixing basic errors.

5. Canva
Canva helps with graphics, documents, and simple visual content. Its AI writing and design features can speed up the blank-page stage significantly.

6. Gamma
Gamma is useful when you need presentations fast. You give it an outline or topic and it helps you build a working first version quickly.

7. Notion AI or similar workspace AI
If you organize work in notes and docs, workspace AI tools can summarize meeting notes, extract tasks, and help you clean up rough drafts.

8. Image generation tool for quick visuals
For creators, an AI image tool can help produce concept art, thumbnails, or background visuals faster. You still need judgment, but it reduces asset-creation time.

9. Recap
The best setup is not using all 7 every day. Pick one for writing, one for research, and one for visuals. That is usually enough to save a lot of time without overcomplicating your workflow.
