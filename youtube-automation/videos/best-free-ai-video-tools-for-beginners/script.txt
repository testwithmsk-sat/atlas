Hook:
If you want to make videos without filming yourself, these AI video tools can help you start faster without a complicated setup.

Main sections:
1. Intro
This video covers beginner-friendly AI video tools for people making faceless content, explainers, Shorts, and simple marketing videos.

2. Canva
Canva is one of the easiest starting points. You can build videos with templates, captions, stock visuals, and AI writing support. It is simple, fast, and beginner-friendly.

3. CapCut
CapCut is great for fast edits, subtitles, Shorts, and simple effects. It is especially useful if you want clean editing without learning a pro tool immediately.

4. InVideo
InVideo can help turn scripts into video drafts faster. It is useful for rough starting points, especially if you want structured scenes and stock footage support.

5. AI voice tools
Pair a video editor with an AI voice tool if you do not want to record narration. That combination is often enough for a faceless channel.

6. Best beginner workflow
A simple setup is this: write script with AI, generate voice if needed, edit in Canva or CapCut, then upload consistently. Do not overcomplicate your stack too early.

7. Recap
For most beginners, Canva plus CapCut is enough. Add AI voice only if your format needs narration. The best tool is the one that helps you publish consistently.
