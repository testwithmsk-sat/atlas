# Monetization Roadmap

## Simple Model

Use three income layers:

1. YouTube ads
2. affiliate commissions
3. a low-cost digital product

## Why This Matters

Getting to `Rs. 1,00,000/month` from ads alone usually takes time. A hybrid model is safer and more realistic.

## Suggested Sequence

### Month 1

- Publish consistently
- focus on CTR and watch time
- include non-spammy calls to action

### Month 2

- start adding affiliate links for relevant AI tools
- test tool comparison videos
- mention one tool naturally in each related video

### Month 3

- apply for monetization when eligible
- create one simple product:
  - prompt pack
  - AI tools list PDF
  - productivity template

### Month 4 and Beyond

- double down on your top 20 percent of topics
- update older winners into new versions
- build playlists around proven themes

## YouTube Monetization Reality

YouTube can reject repetitive or low-effort reused content. AI assistance is fine, but your final videos still need to feel original and useful.

Safer faceless formats:
- tutorials
- comparisons
- list videos
- explainers
- original research summaries

Risky formats:
- copied scripts
- generic slideshow spam
- heavily repeated templates with tiny changes

## Useful Benchmarks

These are not guarantees, just working targets:

- CTR target: `5%+`
- average view duration target for long videos: `35%+`
- Shorts hook target: first `2` seconds feel immediate and clear

## Beginner Affiliate Categories

- AI writing tools
- AI design tools
- resume builders
- productivity apps
- student software

## Simple Revenue Goal Logic

Example mix:
- ads: `Rs. 30,000`
- affiliate: `Rs. 40,000`
- digital product: `Rs. 30,000`

That is more realistic than expecting ads alone to do everything.
