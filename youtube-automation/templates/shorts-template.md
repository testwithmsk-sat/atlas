# Shorts Template

## Formula

`Hook -> One tip or tool -> Why it matters -> CTA`

## Example

### Hook

`This free AI tool can turn your notes into a presentation in minutes.`

### Core

- what it does
- who it is for
- one fast use case

### CTA

`Follow for more free AI tools.`

## Editing Notes

- keep it under 35 seconds
- add large subtitles
- use jump cuts
- start with the strongest sentence
