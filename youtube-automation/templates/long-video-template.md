## Working Title

`[Title]`

## Hook

Open with the result or surprise first.

Example:
`Most people waste hours doing this manually. Here are 5 free AI tools that can do it in minutes.`

## Structure

### Intro

- what the viewer will gain
- why the topic matters now

### Main Points

- tool or point 1
- tool or point 2
- tool or point 3
- tool or point 4
- tool or point 5

### Recap

- who this helps
- best first step

### CTA

Use a light CTA:
`If you want more simple AI tools like this, subscribe for the next one.`

## Visual Plan

- text overlays
- website screenshots
- icons
- stock clips
- simple transitions

## Editing Notes

- cut silence aggressively
- add captions
- keep the first 20 seconds moving
- show proof when possible
