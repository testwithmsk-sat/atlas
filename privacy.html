Privacy Policy

How customer information is handled on The Digital Atlas.

This policy explains what information may be collected when someone visits the site, signs up for freebies, contacts support, or completes a purchase through the website checkout flow.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that customers choose to provide, such as name, email address, and support messages. Checkout-related billing and transaction details may also be processed by website payment and delivery providers used to complete orders securely.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to deliver purchased digital products, respond to support questions, send free resources that a visitor requested, improve the storefront experience, and communicate about products or orders.

3. Third-Party Platforms

Orders may rely on connected payment processors, hosting services, storage providers, or account tools. Those services may process payment, delivery, or customer account information under their own privacy terms. Customers should review those policies before purchasing.

4. Cookies and Analytics

The site may use basic browser storage or future analytics tools to improve performance, remember preferences, or understand page usage. If analytics are added later, this policy should be updated to reflect the tools in use.

5. Email Communication

If a customer signs up for freebies or product updates, their email may be used to send the requested resource and future brand-related communications. They should always have a clear way to unsubscribe if email marketing is added.

6. Data Requests

Customers who need help with their information, support records, or download access can use the contact page. Requests to update or remove stored contact information should be handled there.
