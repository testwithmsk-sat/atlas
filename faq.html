FAQ

Answers for digital product buyers.

Use this page for common questions about instant downloads, file use, purchase support, and what to expect before buying.

How are files delivered?

Products are intended to be delivered digitally after purchase through the linked selling platform or download-access flow.

Are these physical items?

No. These are digital downloads designed for printing, editing, or organizing online unless a listing clearly says otherwise.

What file formats are included?

That depends on the product. Listings may include PDF, editable template files, spreadsheets, or printable resources depending on the item.

Can buyers reuse products?

Standard usage is personal use or single-business use unless a specific commercial license is offered on the product page.

Do you offer refunds?

Because products are digital, refunds are generally limited. Customers should review the refund policy and contact support if there is a genuine delivery or file issue.

How do I ask about custom help or service work?

Use the contact page or the site chat assistant to ask about custom services, support, or pre-sale questions before ordering.
