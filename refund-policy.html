Refund Policy

Refund expectations for instant-download digital products.

Because digital products are delivered immediately, refund handling for The Digital Atlas should be clear, fair, and consistent.

1. Digital Download Policy

Due to the nature of digital products, refunds are generally not offered once a file has been downloaded or access has been granted, except where required by law or where a genuine product defect can be verified.

2. Eligible Cases

A refund or replacement may be considered if a customer receives the wrong file, cannot access the purchased file after reasonable support, or experiences a major issue caused by the product itself.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are typically not provided for change of mind, accidental purchases, misunderstanding of the product description, lack of compatible software, or failure to read the listing details before buying.

4. How To Request Help

Customers should use the contact page with their order details, purchase email, and a short explanation of the issue. Support can then review whether a replacement, clarification, or refund exception is appropriate.
